How It Works

Buy or sell a textbook in four simple steps.

For Buyers

Find the book you need and connect with the seller directly.

🔍

1. Search

Enter your course code (e.g. ENSE 101) or book title in the search bar to find available listings.

📖

2. Browse

Filter by department, condition, and price range to find a book that fits your budget.

📞

3. Contact

Click on a listing and use the seller's contact info to arrange a meeting on campus.

🤝

4. Exchange

Meet the seller in person, inspect the book, and complete the exchange. Save 30–70%!

For Sellers

List your textbook in under a minute and reach campus buyers directly.

📄

1. Post

Click Post in the nav and fill in your book's title, course code, department, condition and price.

👤

2. Add Contact

Enter your name and contact info (email or phone) so buyers can reach you directly.

🕑

3. Wait

Your listing goes live immediately. Interested buyers will contact you using the info you provided.

✅

4. Mark Sold

Once you've completed the exchange, open your listing and click Mark as Sold.

💡 Tip: Using a University of Regina email builds trust with buyers and sellers on campus.
