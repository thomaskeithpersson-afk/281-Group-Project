Safety Tips

Stay safe when buying and selling textbooks on campus.

⚠ Remember: BookExchange connects students directly. Always use common sense and meet in public campus spaces.
🏫

Meet on Campus

Always meet in a well-lit, public area on campus — the library, student union building, or a busy hallway. Never meet off-campus with a stranger.

👀

Inspect Before You Pay

Check the book thoroughly before handing over any money. Confirm the edition, condition, and that all pages are intact.

💰

Cash or E-Transfer Only

Use cash or a trusted e-transfer service. Never wire money, use gift cards, or pay via third-party apps you don't recognize.

🚫

Never Pay in Advance

Do not send payment before meeting and inspecting the book in person. Legitimate sellers will not ask for prepayment.

👥

Bring a Friend

If you feel unsure, bring a classmate or friend along to the exchange. There's safety in numbers.

📧

Use Your U of R Email

Using a uregina.ca email builds trust. It signals you are a real student and makes both parties more accountable.

🔒

Protect Your Info

Only share what's necessary. Your full address and personal details should never be needed for a simple textbook exchange.

🚨

Report Suspicious Behaviour

If something feels off, trust your instincts and walk away. Report suspicious users to campus security or university administration.
