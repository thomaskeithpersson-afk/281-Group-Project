# Team Reflection

## How did you feel about this activity?
This activity was both challenging and rewarding. It pushed our team to turn an idea into a working software prototype and helped us experience the realities of software development under time and resource constraints.

## What did you like about it?
We liked that the project resulted in something tangible and demonstrable. It was satisfying to see the prototype support the main marketplace workflow, including browsing books, posting listings, viewing details, contacting sellers by email, and managing personal listings.

## What did you dislike about it?
The most difficult part was that we had to make trade-offs because of time, complexity, and budget limitations. We could not implement every desired feature in this phase, and running the project only through localhost limited how realistic the deployment experience could be.

## What is your team most proud of?
We are most proud of building a working prototype that demonstrates the core purpose of the platform. Even though the system is not yet fully complete, it successfully shows the main user journey and gives us a strong base for future improvements.

## What did you learn about yourself as you collaborated and worked on this activity?
Individually, team members learned how to better manage their time under pressure and discovered their personal strengths in either front-end design or back-end logic.We learned that collaboration works best when responsibilities are clear and the team agrees early on what is realistic. We also learned that reducing scope is sometimes the best decision if it helps deliver a stable and credible prototype.

## How will you use what you learned going forward?
Going forward, we will use this experience to plan projects more strategically, define scope earlier, and separate essential features from optional enhancements. We will also pay more attention to deployment planning and technical feasibility from the start.

## What "stuff & things" related to this activity would you like more help with?
We would like more help with secure authentication, deployment and hosting decisions, and designing future features such as in-app messaging and peer rating systems.
